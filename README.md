# total-signal-academy

A five-term academy for listening to, filtering, and deciphering electronic signals generated live in the browser via Web Audio.

### Curriculum & Mechanics

* **Term I: Calibration (Timing)**: Calibrate your probe against fluctuating carrier frequencies to achieve phase lock.
* **Term II: Relay (Memory)**: Listen to transmission pulses, commit sequences to memory, and retransmit them without distortion.
* **Term III: Separation (Spectrum)**: Isolate faint transmissions masked inside a crowded radio spectrum using bandpass frequency filters.
* **Term IV: Decipherment (Pattern)**: Decode modulated auditory and visual signals into meaningful telegraphic sequences.
* **Term V: The Archive (Synthesis)**: Reconstruct historical signal fragments under thermal dissipation and signal degradation limits.
* **Audio Synthesis & Visuals**: Pure synthesized sound architecture—no recorded audio files or external dependencies. Features oscilloscopes, glowing spectral waterfalls, and real-time CRT phosphor effects.
